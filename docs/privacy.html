Privacy policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Arc Chrome Tweaks processes browsing information to provide its tab, navigation, and workspace features. This policy describes what the extension accesses and how it is used.

Information the extension accesses

  • Open tab titles and URLs: Used to display and search tabs, switch between them, organize tabs into groups, and provide navigation controls.
  • Current page title and URL: Used by in-page controls and when you invoke URL copy or navigation features.
  • Installed extension details: Chrome’s extension-management API is used to show installed extension information and icons in the interface.
  • Your preferences: Settings are stored using Chrome Storage Sync and Chrome local storage. Some icon data is cached locally.

How information is used and shared

Tab and page information is processed by the extension to provide the features described above. Based on the published source code reviewed for this policy, browsing information is not sent to a server operated by the developer.

To display website and extension icons, the extension may request images from Google’s favicon service. Those requests include the relevant website URL or domain, which Google receives and handles under its own privacy terms. Chrome may synchronize extension settings through your Google account if Chrome Sync is enabled; that service is governed by Google’s policies and settings.

Sale and advertising

The developer does not sell browsing information, use it for advertising, or transfer it to data brokers.

Retention and deletion

Preferences and cached icon data remain in Chrome storage until cleared, removed by the extension, or deleted when you uninstall the extension. Chrome Sync data can also be managed through your Chrome and Google account settings.

Permissions

The extension requests access to storage, tabs, tab groups, sessions, scripting, extension management, and eligible websites. These permissions support settings, tab navigation and grouping, restoring recently closed items, adding the interface to pages, and displaying installed extension details. Chrome-restricted pages do not allow extension content scripts.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the extension’s behavior changes. For questions about this policy or the extension, contact the publisher through GitHub.
